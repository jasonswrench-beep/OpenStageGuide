# The rest of the kit: spec and build order

Written 2026-11-15, after the author's answer "The whole kit. I would rather not push and sign a release until it's done." It covers section 6 of `BUILD-STATUS-CHECKLIST-2026-11-15.md` and the gaps `EXAMPLES-REVIEW-2026-11-13.md` found in 16 real guides, in that review's proposed order. Forms and handouts (`FORMS-AND-HANDOUTS-SPEC-2026-11-13.md`) are done. **No setup is built, signed or published until every slice below is done, or the author says otherwise.**

## How it is built

The same way as the forms: the guide stays a list of styled paragraphs with no hidden record, so each new thing is a paragraph style (or a few), read by the model, listed by a tool beside the guide, checked by the Review, and kept out of the participant workbook when it is for the facilitator (the leak check watches for it). Each slice ends with tests and a real-browser check, and is recorded here under "As built". One new facilitator-only style, **OSG Fact** (a label and its words, "Purpose: ..."), serves the lesson and module headers, the time facts and the planning facts, so the workbook and leak check learn one style, not ten.

Decisions below are the **suggested answers** the forms spec used; the author is working through the whole kit without stopping at each, so they are written down here to be changed, not asked one by one.

## K1. Lesson and module headers

- **What:** each lesson can carry a header under its Time line: *Purpose*, *Materials*, *Method* (one of a short list: lecture, discussion, small-group work, pair work, individual work, demonstration, role play, game, case study, video, break), *Optional*, and *Choose n of m* (a set of blocks or lessons of which the facilitator picks some). A module's overview adds *Key message* and *Teaching aids*. The module overview also lists the slides of its lessons ("Slides: 12"), built from the guide.
- **Stored as:** paragraphs in OSG Fact, one per fact, directly under the lesson's Time line, in a fixed order.
- **Tool:** **Lesson details** (Sections menu): the lesson the cursor is in, its fields, Save writes or replaces the facts. 
- **Pages:** Training at a Glance gets a **Method** column when any lesson has a method; the agenda shows it too.
- **Review:** a lesson with no purpose (a note); a method that is not in the list; *Choose n of m* with n larger than m.
- **Workbook:** none of it reaches the workbook.

## K2. A wider time model

- **What:** a lesson's time may be a **range** ("15 to 20 minutes": the planned minutes are the working number, the range is what the facilitator may take); **Set-up** and **Clean-up** blocks (minutes before the first lesson and after the last); a **stated programme length** and **day starts** in Course settings; an **interaction mode** per lesson (whole group, small groups, pairs, individual, break) shown in the agenda; **group size** as a note on a lesson ("4 to 6 people").
- **Stored as:** *Range*, *Mode* and *Group* facts (K1's style); two new cues, Set-up and Clean-up; the stated length and day starts in the guide's settings (kept like the form settings).
- **Tool:** the same Lesson details tool; the stated length and day starts in **Timing and agenda**, which shows each day from its own start time.
- **Review:** the planned minutes are outside the range; the guide's total differs from the stated length; a day longer than three hours with no break; no stated length at all when the guide has more than two hours (a note).

## K3. Script mechanics

- **What:** new cues **Expected answer** (a "listen for" line after an Ask, facilitator only), **Debrief**, **Option** (substitute or extra material), **Set-up** and **Clean-up**; the **named techniques** (Think-Pair-Share, Gallery Walk, Parking Lot, Exit Ticket, 3-2-1, Jigsaw, Fishbowl) added to the activity library's starter set, each with numbered steps.
- **Review:** an Expected answer with no Ask before it (a note).
- **Workbook:** Expected answer, Option, Set-up and Clean-up are for the facilitator.

## K4. Reference pages

- **What:** a **Glossary** (terms and their meanings, kept in order), **References** (one per line), **figure captions** numbered by Update guide with a source line ("Figure 2.1: Caption. Source: ..."), a **pull quote** with its attribution, and a **licence page** (All rights reserved, CC BY, CC BY-NC, CC BY-SA, CC BY-NC-SA, with the author's own words for permissions) with a **review date** and a **place the law of** line for content that dates.
- **Stored as:** styles for each (OSG Term, OSG Reference, OSG Caption, OSG Quote, OSG Quote By); the licence and review facts in the guide's settings, written to a built page.
- **Tool:** **Reference pages** (Make menu): add a term, a reference, a quote; choose the licence; build the Glossary, References and Licence pages (each only changed when it is built again, like Training at a Glance).
- **Workbook:** the glossary, the references, captions and quotes go in; the licence page goes in; nothing else changes.

## K5. The planning kit

- **What:** form templates for a **planning timeline** (stages from weeks out to after the event, tasks with tick boxes, owner, lead time, notes), **pre-work** and **follow-up** items, a **technology and materials** table with a responsibility column, a **room set-up** page (a picture and do and don't lists), a **roles grid** (lead, table lead, timekeeper); and a built **Materials list** page that collects each lesson's Materials facts (K1) and the copies to make (forms spec F6), grouped for the facilitator, each person and each team, with quantities.
- **Where:** the templates join the Forms tool's list; the Materials list is a new page in **Generate materials**.
- **Facilitator only:** all of it, unless the author marks a form for everyone.

## K6. Layouts for the Word file

- **What:** choices that belong to the output, not the content, kept in the guide's settings and applied when the guide is saved as a Word file: **section dividers** (each module starts a page with its title), a **running header** naming the module, **mirrored footers** with page numbers on the outside, and a **one-page summary** (a generated page for a module or the guide, printed on one side).
- **Tool:** **Layout** in the Make menu.
- **Not in this slice, and said so:** a slide picture beside the script and a margin column of icons need a table-based or frame-based layout for every block; they are listed for later.

## K7. Import

- **What:** **File > Import a Word file...** reads a Word document the add-in (or this program) made: its paragraphs, styles by name, tables and pictures, into a new guide; **File > Paste text...** turns pasted lines into a guide ("Module 1: ..." and "Lesson 1.1: ..." lines become modules and lessons, the rest body text).
- **Stored as:** a new guide beside the others; nothing is written over.

## Outputs the checklist still lists

- **A web page** made from the guide (File > Save as web page), from the program's own view of it.
- **A PDF**: the window's print dialog (File > Print), with the page set up to print the guide; whether the PDF it makes is tagged is checked and stated as it is found, not assumed.
- **Editing surface:** add and delete a table's columns, merged cells, a table's caption and description after it is in, a picture's description, size and caption after it is in.

## What only the author can do

A screen-reader pass; Windows high contrast on a real PC (the program is checked here under forced colors); installing on a second computer; opening the landscape and handout Word files in a real Word.

## Order

K1, K2, K3, K4, K5, K6, K7, then the web page and print, then the editing-surface gaps, then the checklist is brought up to date and the author is asked once for the go to build.

## As built

Written 2026-11-15 as each slice was finished. Every slice has unit tests; the ones with a screen have a check in a real browser as well.

- **K1 Lesson and module headers.** The **Lesson details** tool (Sections menu) writes a lesson's facts under its time line in one paragraph style each (`src/guide-facts.mjs`): purpose, materials, method, range of minutes, interaction, group size, new day, optional and "choose n of m". Facilitator only: the participant workbook leaves them out and the leak check watches them. The Review reads them (a range that does not hold the lesson's minutes, an unknown method, "choose 3 of 2").
- **K2 A wider time model.** A start time and a stated length of the programme; days with a clock that starts again; the agenda reads ranges, set-up before and clean-up after. The Review compares the typed times with the stated length and warns when a day passes three hours with no break.
- **K3 Script mechanics.** Five more block kinds (expected answer, optional, slide or flip chart as one instruction, and two more), five more techniques in the activity library, and their icons (stand-ins drawn here: they are the author's to replace).
- **K4 Reference pages** (`src/guide-reference.mjs`, tool **Reference pages**, Make menu). A glossary and a list of references that keep alphabetical order as entries are added; figure captions with their source that Update guide numbers by module (Figure 2.1); pull quotes with who said them; a licence and permissions page (all rights reserved, four Creative Commons licences or the author's own words), built again over itself. Learners have all of it, in the workbook.
- **K5 The planning kit.** Six more form templates: planning timeline, technology and materials, room set-up and roles grid (facilitator only whatever is ticked), pre-work and follow-up roadmap (for everyone). The built **Materials list** page (Generate materials) gathers each lesson's materials and the copies to make, grouped for the facilitator, each person, each team, each table and the room; it is kept out of the workbook.
- **K6 Layouts for the Word file** (`src/guide-output-layout.mjs`, tool **Layout**, Make menu). Kept with the guide's settings and used when the guide is saved as a Word file: each module on a new page; a running header (the guide's title, or the name of the module, with the front pages left without one); a footer with the page number on the right or on the outside of two-sided pages, with the author's words; and a **one-page summary** (a built page that the Word file puts on a sheet of its own). Not in this version, as the spec said: a picture of each slide beside the script and a column of icons in the margin. *Checked here as a zip, as markup and in the pane; not opened in a real Word.*
- **K7 Import.** **File > Import a Word file...** reads a Word document that this program or the add-in made (paragraphs, styles by name, tables, pictures) and **File > Paste text...** reads "Module" and "Lesson" lines; each opens as a new guide beside the others and nothing is written over. What a Word file holds that the program does not keep (bullets and numbering, comments, notes, headers and footers, styles the guide does not have, tracked changes, which are accepted) is left out and said so. A hostile file is read without harm: fields that reach out, links, objects and pictures that are not pictures are taken out. While doing this a race was found and fixed: a restore or an import was put into the new guide before it had finished opening; the program now waits.
- **Save as web page.** **File > Save as web page...** makes one file from the program's own view of the guide: its style sheet and its pictures are inside, it reaches nowhere, it has a language, a skip link, one main region, headings with addresses and a contents that links to them, a table with its header, a dark setting, a forced-colors setting and a print setting.
- **Print.** **File > Print...** (Ctrl+P) opens the window's print dialog with a print style: the guide alone, on white, over as many sheets as it needs. *What was measured:* the PDF that this engine (Chromium) makes by itself is **not tagged**; asked to tag, it is (structure tree, language). Whether the window's own print dialog saves a tagged PDF was **not** checked (the window was not driven). The way to a tagged PDF that is checked stays the Word file saved as a PDF from Word.
- **Editing surface.** Edit > Add table column right, Delete table column, Join cell with the next, Split joined cell, Table title and description..., and Picture details... (description, decoration, width and caption). Columns keep the table as wide as it was; the header row is not joined (a header that spans columns cannot name them); cells joined downwards are left alone with the reason.

**Not done, and said so:** a slide picture beside the script and the margin icon column (K6); pictures inside table cells; copying a picture between guides; a bullet toggle; dragging a whole module or lesson. Human-only: a screen-reader pass, Windows high contrast on a real PC, installing on a second computer, opening the landscape, handout and layout Word files in a real Word, and trying the window's own print dialog.
